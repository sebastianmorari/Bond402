---
name: Bond402 usage model
description: Durable product boundary between quota-bearing checks and simulations
---

Only productive service checks consume the monthly Bond402 quota: live checks,
manual verification, and AI-agent pre-action decisions. Demo-service additions
and the x402/Bond simulations remain free and do not change usage.

**Why:** The product promise is a Trust Firewall for real agent/API decisions,
while the sandboxes are explicitly educational simulations and must remain safe
to explore without unexpectedly consuming a customer allowance.

**How to apply:** Keep quota enforcement at the real authenticated check routes.
Do not attach usage consumption to demo listing, demo-service creation, or
simulation-only UI flows.
